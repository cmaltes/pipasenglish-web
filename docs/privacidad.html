Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026 · This is a translation; if in doubt, the Spanish version prevails.

Pipas English is an app to practise English vocabulary and grammar for certification exams, made by one person. This page explains what data it uses, what for, and what you can do with it. The short version: only what is needed for the app to work and remember your progress is stored; there are no ads, no analytics, and no data is sold.

Controller

Fernando Munguía Hoyo, Cantabria (Spain). Contact: info@lamaltesa.dev.

What data is processed

  • Your account: your name, your e-mail address and a user identifier. If you sign in with Google, only your e-mail and name are received (scopes email, profile and openid); never your Google password. If you sign in with your e-mail there is no password: a one-time code is sent to you each time.
  • Your preferences: the exam, level and themes you choose, and the interface language.
  • Your progress: each question you answer (question identifier, whether you got it right, when and, where applicable, how long it took) and your estimated level in each theme. It is used to adapt the difficulty, avoid repeats and show you your statistics and streak.
  • Friends (optional): if you open the Friends screen, your name, avatar, streak, seeds eaten, points of the week and badges are published so that your friends can see them, along with the list of your friends. Invitation links hold your name and avatar for 7 days.
  • Doubts about answers (optional): if you press “I think this answer is wrong”, the question identifier, what you chose and your comment are sent to the administrator.
  • Reminders (optional): the daily reminder is a notification created on your own phone; nothing is sent to any server for it.

The app does not use your location, microphone, contacts or photos.

What it is used for

Only to provide the service you ask for: signing in, saving your progress, choosing your next exercises and showing your statistics. Also, in aggregate and without identifying you, to know which themes need more questions (how much of the question bank each person has seen). The legal basis is performance of the service you accept by using the app.

Sign-in with Google

Pipas English's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your e-mail and name are only used to create your account and show you who you are; they are not transferred to third parties, not used for advertising, and no human reads them unless you allow it or it is required for security or by law. You can revoke access in your Google account.

Who else sees it

  • Nobody sees your progress, except the friends you accept: they see your name, avatar, streak, seeds, points of the week and badges, nothing more. There are no public profiles. You can remove a friend at any time.
  • The administrator reads the doubts sent about answers, to correct questions.
  • Providers that host or transmit the data, acting as processors:
    • Amazon Web Services (Cognito, AppSync, DynamoDB and SES): accounts, database and e-mailing of the codes, in the Stockholm (EU) region.
    • Google: sign-in with Google.
    Some of these providers may process data outside the EU under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

No data is sold or passed to anyone else, or used for advertising.

How long it is kept

For as long as you have an account. From the app (home screen → Delete my account) you can delete your account and all your data at any time, and it is removed immediately. If you no longer have the app, see how to delete your account by e-mail.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, object to its processing, restrict it or ask for a copy by writing to info@lamaltesa.dev. If you think your rights have not been respected you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency.

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 14.

Changes

If this policy changes it will be published here with its date and, if the change is significant, announced in the app.
