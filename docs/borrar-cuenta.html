Delete your account

Pipas English, by Fernando Munguía Hoyo · Last updated: 18 October 2026

You can delete your Pipas English account and all your data whenever you want, in two ways.

From the app

  1. Open Pipas English and sign in.
  2. On the home screen tap Delete my account and confirm.

It is deleted at once and you are signed out.

Without the app, by e-mail

If you no longer have the app installed, write to info@lamaltesa.dev from the e-mail address you use in Pipas English, with the subject “Delete my account”. Your account and data will be deleted within 30 days at most and you will get a reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, e-mail address and user identifier.
  • Your preferences (exam, level and themes), your answers and your estimated level per theme.

No copies of your data are kept after deletion.

More information in the privacy policy.
