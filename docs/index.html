How it works

  1. 1

    Choose exam, level and themes

    One or several typical exam themes: travel, work, health, technology, environment, society, culture, daily life…

  2. 2

    Practise with exam-style exercises

    Four-option multiple choice, gaps you fill by typing, word formation, and synonyms and antonyms with a letter hint, sentence ordering and error finding, plus idioms and sayings.

  3. 3

    Learn from every answer

    Answers are marked at once, and each option is explained: why it is right or wrong.

Made at home, for home

If I made Recuérdame for myself, Pipas English is for my wife and my kids, who love learning languages almost as much as eating sunflower seeds. It is made for those short moments when you don't know what to do with yourself:

  • 🚌 waiting for the bus
  • 🛒 in the supermarket queue
  • ☕ with your coffee
  • 🍳 while dinner cooks
  • 🩺 in the waiting room
  • 🚗 as a passenger on a long drive
  • 🛋️ on the sofa, before bed
  • 🌰 with a handful of sunflower seeds

Five minutes, a few questions, and on with your day.
