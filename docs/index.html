How it works

  1. 1

    Take the level test and choose themes

    Twenty questions, no clock, place you between A1 and C2. Then one or several themes: travel, work, health, technology, environment, society, culture, daily life…

  2. 2

    Practise with all kinds of exercises

    Four-option multiple choice, gaps you fill by typing, word formation, and synonyms and antonyms with a letter hint, sentence ordering and error finding, plus idioms and sayings.

  3. 3

    Learn from every answer

    Answers are marked at once, and each option is explained: why it is right or wrong.

Everything you need, nothing more

🎯

Your level, by type of exercise

The app works out your level (A1–C2) in each type of exercise and adjusts it with your answers; practise at your level or pick another one.

📚

Vocabulary you will use

Collocations, phrasal verbs and word formation tied to everyday life, work and current affairs.

🔁

Few repeats

What you haven't seen comes first; a little revision of your mistakes helps it stick.

🔥

Progress and streaks

Your accuracy, your level by type of exercise and how many days in a row you have practised.

🏅

Friends, badges and mock tests

Invite your family with a link, compete in the weekly ranking, earn badges for your streaks and achievements and try a timed mock test.

🔑

No passwords

Sign in with Google or your e-mail: we send you a code each time.

🌍

Built to grow
